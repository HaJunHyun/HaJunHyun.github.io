Hi! I am a master's student at [KAIST AI](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/), advised by [Prof. Juho Lee](https://juho-lee.github.io/).
I am a member of the [SIML](https://siml.kaist.ac.kr/) lab. Before joining KAIST, I received my B.S. in Statistics from Seoul National University in 2025.

My research focuses on **reinforcement learning and policy improvement**.
I am interested in how agents can improve their behavior through data and interaction, and how exploration and learned representations can support this process.
